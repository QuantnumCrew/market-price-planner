<h1>🌾 Agriculture Market Price & Harvest Logistics Planner</h1>

<hr>

<h2>📌 Problem Statement</h2>
<p>
Farmers often face uncertainty after harvest due to <strong>fluctuating market prices</strong>, <strong>transport costs</strong>, <strong>storage limitations</strong>, and <strong>crop shelf-life</strong> constraints. Selling at the wrong time or in the wrong market can reduce profits significantly.  
This project provides a <strong>data-driven AI solution</strong> to help farmers decide <strong>when and where to sell crops</strong> for maximum net revenue while minimizing storage loss and transport costs.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>Net revenue comparison across markets and timeframes</li>
<li>Transport cost estimation based on distance</li>
<li>Storage loss calculation considering shelf-life</li>
<li>Dashboard visualization with actionable recommendations</li>
<li>Simple route plan and farmer checklist</li>
<li>Risk alerts for price volatility and storage loss</li>
<li>Exportable outputs (CSV / JSON) for integration</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>📥 Input / 📤 Output</h2>

<h3>Inputs:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Daily market prices (mock or API-ready)</li>
<li>Transport distance & rate</li>
<li>Storage capacity & loss rate</li>
<li>Crop quantity & shelf-life</li>
</ul>

<h3>Outputs:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Recommended selling strategy (sell now vs later)</li>
<li>Net revenue comparison table</li>
<li>Route plan and checklist</li>
<li>Risk notes and assumptions</li>
<li>Exportable CSV / JSON files</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>🛠️ Tech Stack</h2>
<ul>
<li>Python – Core logic</li>
<li>Pandas, NumPy – Data processing</li>
<li>Streamlit – Dashboard & UI</li>
<li>CSV / JSON – Structured output</li>
<li>Mock data for market prices (extendable to real APIs)</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>🔐 Ethics & Safety</h2>
<ul>
<li>Uses synthetic or consented data only</li>
<li>No medical, chemical, or pesticide advisories</li>
<li>Disclaimers included for price uncertainty</li>
<li>Encourages escalation to agricultural experts for high-risk cases</li>
<li>Privacy-first design principles</li>
</ul>
